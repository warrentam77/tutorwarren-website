EAL Level 3 Electrical Installation · Exam revision suite

The exam

On-screen MCQ and advanced question types · closed book · 30 questions · 60 minutes. Pass mark normally around 50%. Non-programmable calculator allowed. One re-take only, and the centre-marked assignment must also be passed.

Where the marks are

Exam technique

Allow about 2 minutes per question. Write down the formula, convert units first (kW to W, µF to F, mm² etc.), then calculate. Use BODMAS. On calculation MCQs, check your answer is sensible, as wrong options are often the result of common slips such as forgetting √3 or using poles instead of pole pairs.

Mock exam

30 questions, weighted to match the real paper (2/4/7/5/7/2/3). 60-minute timer. Pass at 50%.
