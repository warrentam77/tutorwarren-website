Exam technique

Allow about 2 minutes per question. Write down the formula, convert units first (kW to W, µF to F, mm² etc.), then calculate. Use BODMAS. On calculation MCQs, check your answer is sensible, as wrong options are often the result of common slips such as forgetting √3 or using poles instead of pole pairs.
