Welcome

This interactive lesson covers the fire triangle, fire classes, extinguisher colour bands, safe intervention, evacuation, emergency calls, PASS technique, prevention and smoke safety.

Golden rule: life safety comes first. Do not risk injury to save property.

1. The fire triangle

Heat

Energy that raises fuel to ignition temperature: flame, hot surface, electrical fault or friction.

Fuel

Anything that can burn: solids, liquids, gases, cooking oils and some metals.

Oxygen

Usually supplied by air. Smothering reduces the oxygen available.

3. Safe intervention

  1. Only a trained or instructed person should consider it.
  2. The fire must be small, contained and at an early stage.
  3. The alarm must be raised and evacuation underway.
  4. The correct extinguisher must be immediately available.
  5. A clear escape route must remain behind you.
  6. Abandon the attempt if the fire grows, smoke increases or escape is threatened.
Evacuate instead if untrained, unsure, alone, trapped, affected by smoke, or facing gas, chemicals, high voltage, cooking oil or rapid spread.

4. Fire procedure

  1. Raise the alarm and shout “Fire!”.
  2. Call 999: give the exact address, location, material burning and anyone missing.
  3. Evacuate by the nearest safe exit; do not use lifts unless directed.
  4. Close doors behind you if safe, without locking them.
  5. Go to the assembly point and report to the responsible person.
  6. Never re-enter until authorised as safe.
If trapped: close the door, block smoke gaps if possible, call 999, give your location and signal at a window.

5. PASS technique

P – Pull

Pull the pin or remove the seal.

A – Aim

Aim at the base of the flames.

S – Squeeze

Squeeze the operating handle.

S – Sweep

Sweep from side to side while keeping your escape route.
